Ordering takes four steps

  1. Install the Yellow Ribbon app on your Android phone and create an account.
  2. Pick your pizzas and drop a pin where you want them delivered.
  3. Pay the way you like: cash when it arrives, GCash or Maya.
  4. Follow your rider on the map and get a notification when your order is on its way.
